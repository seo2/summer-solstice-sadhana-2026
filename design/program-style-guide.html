11 Base effects

The cross-cutting rules that make everything look like one family.

Glass

Translucent white 0.82–0.96 + backdrop-filter: blur(18–20px) + 1px blue border at 10–12%.

Shadow

Always blue-tinted and large: 0 18px 48px rgba(47,98,182,.08); on strong cards 0 24px 70px …/.10.

Radii

rounded-2xl = 16px (cards), rounded-xl = 12px (inputs/photos), rounded-full (pills/chips/favorite).

Radial glows

Peach/gold in the top-right corner, sky in the bottom-left. Decorative, low opacity, inside the card.

Motion

Minimal: 160ms transitions on pills and :active { transform: scale(.98) }. No keyframes.

Rhythm

Container max-width: 48rem, 1rem side padding. Between cards of the same day: 0.75rem. Inner padding 1rem (list) / 1.5rem (detail).
